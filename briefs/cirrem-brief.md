# CIRREM - Synthesis Brief

## Core Identity

CIRREM is a late-winter, self-supported 100-kilometer gravel race that starts
and finishes at Cumming Tap in Cumming, Iowa. The hilly course uses open rural
roads through Madison County. The organizer's archive runs from 2009 through
the 18th edition on February 28, 2026.

## Verified Facts

- **Location:** Cumming, Iowa; Madison County
- **Distance:** 100 kilometers, about 62 miles
- **Elevation:** Not published by the organizer
- **Terrain:** Hilly gravel roads; surface can be frozen, loose, icy, muddy, or thawing
- **Format:** Self-supported, with a checkpoint roughly halfway
- **Start:** 2026 edition started at 10:00 AM after breakfast and rider check-in
- **Weather:** Snow, freezing rain, sleet, ice, wind, and thawing gravel all appear in the official results history
- **Next edition:** No 2027 date or registration cycle announced as of August 15, 2026

## Course Character

The physical course is rolling rather than mountainous. Its defining demand is
the combination of repeated hills, open-road self-support, and late-February
weather. The organizer warns about loose or slippery gravel, high speeds,
vehicle traffic, and wood-deck bridges with gaps that can catch a wheel.
