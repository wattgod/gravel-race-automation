# Gravel Fever - Synthesis Brief

## Current Status

The planned October 23-25, 2026 Gravel Fever festival in Châtellerault was
cancelled after Grand Châtellerault ended its financial support. France Vélo
Événements is seeking a replacement host, but no replacement edition, date, or
route has been announced. Do not publish or sell a race-dated training plan
until those details are official.

## Historical Identity

Gravel Fever was a multi-format French gravel festival based in Châtellerault.
Its 2025 edition hosted the French Gravel Championships and drew more than 1,000
participants across formats.

## Historical Flagship Course

- **Location:** Châtellerault, France
- **Distance:** 155.1 km / 96.4 miles
- **Elevation:** 1,522.8 m / 4,996 feet
- **Terrain:** Rolling western-France gravel, rural tracks, and paved links
- **Route basis:** 2025 championship route, RideWithGPS 52592435
- **Status:** Historical reference only; a new host may produce a different course

## Publication Rule

Retain the historical race page with a prominent cancellation notice. Exclude
Gravel Fever from the active TrainingPeaks pipeline, countdown feed, plan CTAs,
and publication queue until an organizer announces a replacement host, exact
date, race format, and official route.
