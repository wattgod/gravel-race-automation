# Gravel God blog performance

## How to read this

- **Main comparison: week over week.** This week = 2026-10-12 → 2026-10-18; last week = 2026-10-05 → 2026-10-11. Δ % compares views per usable day.
- **Usable day** = on or after the page type's tracking start and not an excluded date. The Days column says how many each week had.
- **WordPress posts: tracking began 2026-10-09.** Their earlier numbers are not a baseline (no GA4 tag), so they are never compared against days before that.
- **Excluded dates (scripted QA traffic):** 2026-10-09 (26 views), 2026-10-10 (4 views). Change with `--exclude-dates`.
- Headless filter: applied (every query drops rows whose GA4 browser begins with "Headless"), but GA4 reported 0 such views: the QA runs are recorded as ordinary browsers, so only the date exclusion removes them.
- **Recommended:** in GA4, define internal traffic (Admin → Data streams → Configure tag settings → Define internal traffic) and activate the Internal Traffic data filter, so QA runs stop landing in reports.
- "–" means no usable days, or no views to compute a rate from.
