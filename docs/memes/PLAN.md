# Gravel God meme illustrations: plan

2026-10-09. Matt, on the essay prototype: "the illustrations should also lean heavily on common internet memes or instagram memes recreated with the character, if possible, if it makes sense in the context of the article or headline it's illustrating."

Every meme is drawn from scratch on the locked v6.2 rig (`scripts/drill_video/essay_memes.py`, branch `feat/essay-memes`). Each one copies a format's composition and nothing else: no photo, still or original art is traced or copied, and no character from the source (Drake, Spider-Man, Gru, Meme Man, the anime guy) is drawn. Gravel God plays every role. The Impact-style captions use **Anton** (SIL OFL, bundled in `scripts/drill_video/fonts/`) instead of Monotype's Impact, so the type is licensed for a commercial site. Text is drawn in our palette: paper `#f5efe6` with ink `#3a2e25` outlines, not pure white on black.

Captions are our own words and say only what the essays say. No coach or brand is named beyond the essay's own text (the memes name none).

## Legal and other risks

- **Composition only.** Every format here is a widely parodied layout. We redraw the layout with our own character, setting and words, and nothing is traced. The remaining risk is low, and it is reputational more than legal (people recognise the format, which is the point).
- **Drake / Spider-Man / Gru / Stonks.** None of the source characters, likenesses, costumes or logos appear. We don't use the formats' names in on-page text or alt text beyond a generic description.
- **Real names.** The memes name no coach, company or product. The training-app memes say "YOUR TRAINING APP", not a brand.
- **Numbers.** Every number comes from the essays: 235 W, 94%, 215 W, +30 W, 10-week, 91%, 3×20.
- **Font.** Anton (OFL 1.1) is redistributable and fine for commercial raster output; its licence sits next to the font.

## Render notes

- Phone layouts are recomposed, not cropped: 660×825 (Spider-Man, Stonks, Gru, pigeon), 660×990 (Panik/Kalm), 660×660 (both Drakes).
- `rsvg-convert` on macOS ignores `fonts/` unless `PANGOCAIRO_BACKEND=fc` is set (pango uses CoreText otherwise). `essay_memes.py` sets it. `essay_scenes.py` doesn't, so the tablet's "86–92%" and the tombstone lettering in the earlier renders probably used fallback fonts, not Source Serif 4 / Sometype Mono.
- Faces are drawn with `head_tilt` 0 (except the Drake "no" panel). At close-up zoom a tilt cross-fades two painted pitch views and leaves a visible ghost of the face.

## Design review fixes (2026-10-09)

Review: `../sol-memes/sol.md`. Kept as rendered: #2 Panik/Kalm, #4 Gru, #5 Drake polarized, #8 Drake fit.

- **#1 spiderman-threshold (factual fix).** The right label said "100% of your real FTP", which overstates the case study. The essay's numbers: tested FTP 250 W, 3×20 at 235 W (94% of tested); real threshold drifts 235 / 228 / 220 / 215 W across the session, so 235 W is 100 / 103 / 107 / 109% of it. The label now reads "100–109% of your real threshold". The phone version breaks both small labels onto two lines so they render at about 20 px at 390 px wide (they were about 11 px). The pointing hands are redrawn in `essay_memes.py` only (a fist with knuckles and a thumb, and an index finger with a rounded tip). The rig's own `draw_hand` is unchanged, so the kept memes are untouched.
- **#3 stonks-tss (satire, not evidence).** The grid is gone. The chart has bare axes with arrowheads, no numbers, and the word "WEEKS OF SWEET SPOT" (phone: "WEEKS") on the x-axis. The lines are labelled in big type, "WEEKLY TSS" and "ACTUAL ADAPTATION", and a red tag reads "TSS IS NOT FITNESS". The tag spells the words out because Anton's ≠ glyph renders without a visible slash, so "TSS ≠ FITNESS" read as "TSS = FITNESS".
- **#6 pigeon-compliance (format restored).** The old version had a symmetric butterfly with check marks that floated like a logo. Now GG stands on the left with his arm out, pointing at a butterfly that is fluttering just past his finger (a three-quarter view with foreshortened far wings, wing veins and flap marks). The butterfly is labelled "91% COMPLIANCE", the character is labelled "YOUR TRAINING APP" across his chest, and "IS THIS RACE PREP?" runs along the bottom. Everything is drawn from scratch, with nothing traced.
- **#7 spiderman-three: cut.** Its outputs and its MAP.json and ALT.txt entries are removed, and so is its code in `essay_memes.py`. The essay section keeps its text with no illustration.
