# 3RIDES Gravel Winterberg — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- 3RIDES Gravel Winterberg runs 17-19 July 2026 in Winterberg as a three-stage gravel race, introduced in the fifth year of 3RIDES.
- Stage 1 is a 14 km gravel time trial with 300 m of climbing, one lap, about 90 percent gravel.
- Stage 2 is the Winterberg North Loop: 70 / 105 km and 1,700 / 2,550 m over two or three 35 km laps, passing the Kahler Asten at almost 842 m.
- Stage 3 is the final and the UCI Gravel World Series race: 86 / 129 km and 2,000 / 3,000 m over two or three laps.
- The event is a UCI Gravel World Series qualifier; 3RIDES previously ran in Aachen.
