# Birkebeinerrittet Sykkel source review

Verified: 2026-08-15

## Edition identity

- Birken's official all-races calendar lists Birkebeinerrittet on Saturday,
  August 28, 2027.
- It is a timed and ranked mountain-bike race from Rena to Håkons Hall in
  Lillehammer.
- The detailed event page still describes the August 29, 2026 edition. Its
  waves, fees, aid service, cutoff times, transport timetable, and other
  operating details are not projected onto 2027.

## Current course contract

The organizer's current route is 84 kilometers with approximately
1,200–1,300 meters of climbing. It starts in central Rena and finishes at
Håkons Hall. The official course description supports this sequence:

- about 10 kilometers of paved opening road before broad gravel;
- Vivelstadsvea and Åstadalen into the Djuposet hike-a-bike;
- forest and gravel roads through the middle of the course;
- Rosinbakkene, Aksjøen, Elgåsen, and the approximately 955-meter Storåsen
  high point; and
- mixed gravel, trail, fast pavement, sharp turns, and Ballettbakken on the
  final descent to Lillehammer.

The old profile's Raudfjellet and Midtfjellet suffering zones were not
supported by the organizer's current route pages and were removed.

## Backpack, aid, and point-to-point operations

The current rules require a conventional shoulder-strap backpack containing
specified safety clothing and equipment. It must weigh at least two kilograms
throughout the race. The current operating page also describes staffed food
stations, participant-and-bike buses from Oslo and Lillehammer to Rena, and
luggage transport from the start to the finish.

Those systems make the present course trainable, but their 2027 schedule,
prices, products, cutoff times, and detailed rules are not yet published. The
final 2027 rider document controls.

Source:

- https://birken.no/en/cycling/birkebeinerrittet-84-km

## History

Birken dates the first race to 1993 with 1,327 participants and describes
Birkebeinerrittet as one of the world's largest mountain-bike races, reaching
roughly 17,000 participants at its peak. The mandatory backpack represents
the infant heir Håkon Håkonsson from the Birkebeiner story.

Source:

- https://birken.no/en/about-birken/the-birkebeiner-history/history-birkebeinerrittet
