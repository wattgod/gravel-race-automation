## Identity correction

The catalog slug `bootlegger-100` refers to Pisgah Productions' gravel cycling
race in Lenoir, North Carolina. The old `bootlegger100.com` domain now promotes
an unrelated trail-running race in Flovilla, Georgia. It is not the source for
this cycling profile.
