# BWR Mexico — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- Gran Fondo Guide's 2025 preview calls the third edition of BWR Mexico "the largest gravel race in Mexico" and says the 2024 edition was the largest gravel race in Mexican history with well over 550 riders.
- Gran Fondo Guide's 2025 race report says the third edition was the largest gravel race in Mexico and carried the largest prize pool of the 2025 BWR series.
- The BWR event page says the 2025 edition brought together nearly 700 participants and describes BWR Mexico as the largest gravel cycling event in the country.
- The race is held in the Tequisquiapan wine and cheese region of Querétaro and is the final stop on the BWR calendar.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- "Largest" is the organizer's and the race press's own billing; no independent census of Mexican gravel events was found. The profile already words it as "billed as".
