# Falling Leaves Lahti — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- Falling Leaves is listed on the Gravelking UCI Gravel World Series calendar for Saturday 12 September 2026 in Lahti, Finland, with 100-175 km distances.
- The UCI Gravel World Series page calls it the iconic event in the Nordic Gravel Series; Lahti hosts the 2027 UEC European Gravel Championships on 31 July 2027.
- The long course has 1,586 m of climbing and the short course 1,044 m; 13 percent of the course is asphalt.
- After 55 km the course turns north into about 70 km of hardpack rollers with no flat sections; a 35 km northern loop runs to and from Uimila.
