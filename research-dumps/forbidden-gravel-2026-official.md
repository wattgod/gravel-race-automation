# Forbidden Gravel — 2026 official fact check

Reviewed: 2026-08-05

## Publishable facts

- The organizer's 2026 series announcement fixes Forbidden Gravel on Sunday,
  September 20, 2026 in Cumberland, British Columbia.
- The announcement offers three approximate distances: 40 km Impulso, 75 km
  Intermedio, and 110 km Esperto.
- The 2026 route is being redesigned and expanded along the Puntledge River,
  with a climb up Forbidden Plateau Road.
- The organizer describes all three distances as supported with aid stations.

## Explicit unknowns

- The final 2026 GPX has not been published.
- The organizer has not published a 2026 elevation total or named segment
  sequence.
- The 2025 RideWithGPS route is historical and must not be presented as the
  2026 course.

These unknowns do not prevent an honest six-week emergency plan: the date,
long-course distance, location, support model, and defining course corridor
are official. Copy and guide material must describe climbing in qualitative
terms and state that the final vertical profile remains pending.
