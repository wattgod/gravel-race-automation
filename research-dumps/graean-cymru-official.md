# Graean Cymru — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- Graean Cymru is the only Welsh event in the Gravelking UCI Gravel World Series, run by the Glorious Gravel team, on 5-6 September 2026 at Llyn Brenig near Ruthin.
- The 57.67 km lap is ridden twice as the official qualifier distance for the UCI Gravel World Championships; one lap has 1,059 m of climbing, with elevation between 300 and 470 m.
- The course runs through Clocaenog Forest; women and men race on separate days.
- The 2025 edition drew 1,000 riders from 30 countries.
