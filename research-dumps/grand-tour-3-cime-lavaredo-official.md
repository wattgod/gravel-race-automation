## Publishable facts

- Grand Tour 3 Cime Lavaredo is a Gravelking UCI Gravel World Series event on 20 June 2026 in Auronzo di Cadore, organized by the team that ran the 2023 UCI Gravel World Championships.
- The inaugural edition drew 700 riders from 28 nations; the long course was 133 km with 3,170 m and the medium course 105 km with 2,460 m.
- Named climbs: Danta di Cadore (1,441 m), Klammbach (1,929 m, highest point), Haunold (1,362 m), Cimabanche (1,526 m), Passo Tre Croci (1,795 m).
- Fabian Rabensteiner won the men's elite race; Karolina Migon won the women's race on the medium course.
