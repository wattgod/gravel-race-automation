# GRAVEL N GRANITE — COMMUNITY RESEARCH

## Source boundary

This research dump covers Gravel n Granite in Tenterfield, New South Wales. Earlier versions accidentally included reports from the Ironbull Granite Grinder in Wausau, Wisconsin. Those unrelated claims and links have been removed.

Current 2027 facts come from the organizer homepage. Rider observations below are historical and are not represented as a final 2027 route specification.

## Organizer-confirmed 2027 facts

- Event weekend: March 5–7, 2027.
- Gravel-course day: Saturday, March 6, 2027.
- Flagship: 91-kilometre Range Rumble.
- Other published distances: 66 kilometres, 38 kilometres, and an 11-kilometre family course.
- Format: adventure enduro on fast gravel and rugged terrain with big views.
- Broad demands: big climbs, fast descents, and National Park backroads.
- Exact elevation, GPX files, start times, aid, cutoff, registration price, field, and awards are not yet published.

Source: https://www.gravelngranite.com.au/

## Historical rider observations

### 2023 long-course briefing and footage

Organizer footage from the Tenterfield event described a 105-kilometre historical long route marked with red arrows. The briefing warned about a newly surfaced and slippery Kildare Road, wet causeways, cattle grids, wildlife, and livestock. On-course footage showed strong wind, multiple feed stations, steep late climbing, and a return over Mount Mackenzie. These observations establish recurring local demands but do not prove the final 2027 course.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpSdn0ZoQ0A

### 2022 participant report

A participant completed the historical 65.6-kilometre course on a mountain bike in 3:42. She reported corrugations, gravel, water crossings, a very steep climb that forced some riders to walk, open roads with little traffic, and two feed stations on that edition. She characterized the event as welcoming to riders treating it as a challenge rather than an all-out race. These distance and aid details belong only to that historical edition.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1N2RdFtlI0

### 2023 event recap

Saddlers MTB Club footage identifies historical 38-, 66-, and 91-kilometre courses plus an 11-kilometre family ride. Participants emphasized granite-country scenery, a mix of challenge and inclusion, and a well-run community weekend. The published distances align with the current 2027 menu, but the footage does not prove identical route files.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4JAzQBHJdk

## 2025 event context

A 2025 preview described a timed but inclusive adventure format, with gravel bikes, mountain bikes, e-bikes, and adaptive bikes welcome. It described new routes after local roads were sealed, a steep Range Rumble crux, mixed gravel and bitumen, stocked support points, a Race Village, and a family-oriented weekend. Those operational and route details are useful history only; the organizer has not yet republished them as 2027 facts.

Sources:

## Training implications supported by the evidence

- Build durable climbing torque for the organizer-promised big climbs.
- Practice descending on fast gravel while fatigued.
- Prepare for corrugations, wet crossings, and changing surface grip as historical possibilities, not guaranteed 2027 features.
- Carry enough fuel and fluid to bridge a conservative interval until the 2027 aid map is published.
- Treat the 91-kilometre duration as official, but do not prescribe an exact elevation profile or mile-specific attack plan yet.
