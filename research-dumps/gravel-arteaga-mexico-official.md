# Gravel Arteaga Mexico — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- The UCI Gravel World Series calls UCI Gravel Arteaga the first-ever UCI Gravel World Series event in Mexico, on Sunday 30 August 2026 in Arteaga, Coahuila, in the Sierra Madre Oriental.
- Gran Fondo: 135 km (calendar header says 85-133 km), about 2,200 m of climbing, starting near 1,800 m and reaching above 2,700 m, with a long early ascent over the first 40 km; qualifier for men 19-59 and women 19-49.
- Medio Fondo: 85 km, about 1,200 m; qualifier for men 60+ and women 50+.
- The same page's profile section gives 1,648 m for the full course and 854 m for the medium course, so the official climbing figures conflict.

## Conflicts and unknowns
