# UCI Gravel Chile — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- UCI Gravel Chile is organized for the first time, at Lago Ranco in Chile's Los Ríos region, by the team running the UCI Gran Fondo World Series races in Brazil and Colombia.
- It is planned as a combined UCI Gravel World Series and UCI Gran Fondo World Series weekend.
- The 100 km course starts at 73 m beside Lago Ranco, climbs 4 km in two steps to the highest point (260 m) at 13.5 km, then runs a 67 km counterclockwise loop toward Río Bueno; total 665 m. The 58 km mediofondo (men 60+, women 50+) has 515 m.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- As of this review the UCI Gravel World Series event page and calendar list the date as Postponed.
