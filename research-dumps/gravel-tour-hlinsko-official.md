Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- Gravel Tour Hlinsko is a new event on the Gravelking UCI Gravel World Series calendar, on Saturday 1 August 2026 in Hlinsko, Czechia; it first ran in 2019 and has hosted the start of the Czech Gravel Championship three times.
- Format: an 86 km main loop for all categories plus a 43 km second loop for elites and age groups up to 49, for 129 km total.
- Climbing is 1,577 m for the full course and 1,102 m for older categories, with a high point of 774 m in the first half; terrain mixes gravel roads, forest tracks and rolling climbs between the Iron Mountains and Žďárské Vrchy.
