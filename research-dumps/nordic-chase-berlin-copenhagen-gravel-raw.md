# NORDIC CHASE BERLIN TO COPENHAGEN GRAVEL - 2027 SOURCE AUDIT

Research date: 2026-08-15

## Identity decision

Nordic Chase Berlin to Copenhagen Gravel is a distinct unsupported gravel
ultra, not an alternate distance of the Copenhagen-to-Oslo race. The latest
detailed route runs from Berlin through northern Germany, crosses the Baltic
by ferry, and finishes in Copenhagen.

## Current official facts

- The Nordic Chase home page lists a 2027 Berlin-to-Copenhagen gravel event
  window of July 31-August 5 and says the four-race series uses a 100-hour
  format.
- The detailed route page still describes the completed 2026 edition: 730 km,
  4,000 m of climbing, more than 60 percent gravel, and a Puttgarden-Rodby
  ferry crossing.
- The latest detailed edition had a 100-rider capacity, GPS tracking, a race
  manual, self-supported resupply, and no organizer aid stations.
- The organizer says 2027 registration opens September 8, 2026 at 19:00 CET,
  with priority access 48 hours earlier.

## Community and secondary-source check

- The organizer's 2025 r/ultracycling post says Nordic Chase began as a small
  Copenhagen-loop event and presents Berlin-to-Copenhagen as a new chapter,
  supporting a conservative prestige and field-depth grade.
- Bikepacking.com's 2026 listing independently matches the 730 km, 4,000 m,
  unsupported format, ferry crossing, 100-hour limit, and 100-rider cap.
- The published 2026 rider list shows an international small field. It does not
  establish a long elite history or support a national-prestige score.

## What is not established

- No exact 2027 grand-depart date or start time is published.
- No final 2027 GPX, race manual, or route-specific cutoff is published.
- No defensible gravel-specific DNF rate, completed-rider consensus, or deep
  elite-field history was found in the sources reviewed.
- No published cash prize purse was found.

## Scoring consequences

The 66/100 T2 grade uses the latest official route only as a guarded planning
reference. Length and elevation meet the published maximum-score thresholds;
prestige, field depth, community, race quality, and expenses stay conservative
because this is a young, small, costly point-to-point event.

## Training-plan consequence

Do not clone, date, or publish a 2027 plan until the organizer publishes the
exact grand depart and final route package. The course history supports a
future FULL-7 ultra ladder, but not a dated 2027 TrainingPeaks calendar today.
