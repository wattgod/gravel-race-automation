# OG Classique — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- OG Classique in Wakefield, Quebec, is a UCI Gravel World Series qualifier; the 2026 edition on 21 June was its first.
- The organizer reports 519 registered riders for the inaugural 2026 edition; Haley Smith and Adam Roberge won the elite races.
- Both 2026 elite races were decided in four-rider sprints on the 124 km course with over 1,600 m of climbing, including the Paugan Dam crossing.
- 2026 routes: Lac Ste-Marie 124 km / 1,548 m / 71 percent gravel; Paugan 75 km / 1,043 m; Newcommon 34 km / 337 m. The 2027 edition is set for 26 June 2027 with course details under review.

## Conflicts and unknowns
