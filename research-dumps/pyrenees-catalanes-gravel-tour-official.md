# Pyrénées Catalanes Gravel Tour — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- The Pyrénées Catalanes Gravel Tour is a UCI Gravel World Series event on Saturday 26 September 2026 in Font-Romeu, France, organized by the team behind 66 Degrés Sud – Le Cyclo (UCI Gran Fondo World Series since 2022).
- It ran in Les Angles in 2025 and moves to Font-Romeu for its second edition.
- The granfondo (qualifier for elites, men 19-59, women 19-49) covers three loops: east via Mont-Louis and Col du Calvaire (1,856 m), south via Eyne 2600, and north to Pic de la Calma (2,006 m) with 12 km to go; finish after 93 km. The UCI page gives 2,033 m of climbing.
- The mediofondo (men 60+, women 50+) skips the northern loop, with 1,726 m. Start altitude is about 1,760-1,800 m.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- The UCI page gives 2,033 m of climbing for the granfondo. The profile's 2,286 m figure does not appear on either fetched page; this review did not fetch the profile's cited Strava route, so that figure is unverified here.
