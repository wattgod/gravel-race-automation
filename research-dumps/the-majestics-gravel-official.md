# The Majestics Gravel — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- The Majestics Gravel is a Gravelking UCI Gravel World Series qualifier based in Villars-sur-Ollon, Switzerland, part of a three-day road-and-gravel festival; it was formerly Gravel Suisse.
- The gravel races start from UCI headquarters in Aigle and climb into the Vaud Alps: 104 km Granfondo (men 19-59, women 19-49) and 72 km Mediofondo (men 60+, women 50+).
- Villars-sur-Ollon has been selected to host the 2029 UCI Gravel World Championships.
