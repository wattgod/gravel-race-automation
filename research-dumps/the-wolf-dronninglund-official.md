# The Wolf (Ulven Gravl) — official sources check

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Built from pages fetched on the review date. Each fact below is stated on at least one listed source.

## Publishable facts

- Ulven Gravl (The Wolf) was a recreational event that became a competitive UCI Gravel World Series event around Dronninglund in northern Jutland, on Saturday 5 September 2026.
- It is the only Danish event on the 2026 UCI Gravel World Series calendar.
- One lap is 52 km with 466 m of climbing; younger riders do three laps and older riders two. The loop starts in Flauenskjold and passes the moat and courtyard of Voergaard Castle early.
- The calendar header lists 82 - 123 - 165 km, which does not match three 52 km laps.
