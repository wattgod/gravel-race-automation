# UEC Gravel European Championships 2027 — Lahti research brief

Reviewed: 2026-08-05

## What is confirmed

- The UEC awarded the 2027 Gravel European Championships to Lahti, Finland. This is the first UEC European Championship staged in Finland.
- The organiser publishes an event window of July 30 through August 1, 2027. The UCI Gravel World Series page identifies the championship race date as July 31, 2027.
- The event is also a 2027 UCI Gravel World Championships qualifier.
- UEC says the course will contain more than 90 percent gravel through the Salpausselka UNESCO Global Geopark, using hardpack gravel, fast dual-track forest roads, and wide flow trails with limited climbing.
- The exact 2027 distance, elevation, course sequence, aid plan, and start schedule are not published. The event site advertises a September 10, 2026 course-recon ride and says more information will follow.
- European-title eligibility requires qualifying in the top 25 percent of an eligible European UCI Gravel World Series event, European sporting nationality, an annual licence, and a UCI ID. Amateurs may enter the sportive with a day licence but cannot contest a European title.
