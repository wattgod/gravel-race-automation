Facts & History

In 1997, Johannesburg's 94.7 Highveld Stereo radio station partnered with the Midrand Country Cyclists club to create a top-tier mass participation cycling event that would capture the city's competitive temperament. The first edition drew 4,500 cyclists, making it the biggest cycling event in Gauteng. It grew rapidly, becoming the world's second-largest timed cycle race. The event has evolved through several naming sponsors: 94.7 Highveld Stereo Cycle Challenge, Momentum 94.7, Telkom 947, Discovery 947, and currently Virgin Active 947 Ride Joburg. The route shifted to start and finish at FNB Stadium in Soweto's Nasrec precinct from 2021, connecting the race more directly to the broader Johannesburg community. Founded in 1997. By 947 Radio (94.7 Highveld Stereo) and Midrand Country Cyclists.

Reputation: South Africa's premier road cycling event. A civic institution in Johannesburg that transcends sport. The race has launched careers, raised millions for charity, and given 20,000+ riders per year the right to own the city's streets.

1997: First edition with 4,500 riders, Gauteng's biggest cycling event
1999: Robert Hunter wins before his successful European professional career
2006: Martin Velits wins - future Giro d'Italia stage racer
2014: Marianne Vos wins the women's race - the greatest female cyclist in history
2017: Daryl Impey wins - Tour de France stage winner and South African cycling legend
2020: COVID-era adaptation keeps the event alive
2021: Route flipped to start/finish at FNB Stadium in Nasrec/Soweto
2024: Ryan Gibbons (Lidl-Trek) wins - current South African road champion

The Course

A rolling 97km urban road race at altitude through Johannesburg's streets, highways, and suburbs. The course is deceptively challenging - the constant altitude above 1,500m and rolling M1 highway sections wear riders down more than the elevation numbers suggest.

Signature challenge: The M1 highway. Seventeen kilometers of rolling highway that car drivers barely notice but cyclists feel in their legs. At 1,500m+ altitude, with 60km already in the legs, the false flats and headwinds of the M1 South section separate those who paced well from those who went out too hard on the Kyalami circuit.

RIDERS REPORT
Heat Management
Temperatures start at 18 degrees in the morning, rise to 25 degrees mid-morning, then up to 33 degrees when most cyclists are expected to finish. Riders describe this as the last day of a heat wave.
Reverse Route
The route is anti-clockwise and completely new - no one can predict finishing times because it's never been raced this direction before.

Race Logistics

Airport
OR Tambo International Airport (JNB) - 30 minutes to Johannesburg CBD, 45 minutes to FNB Stadium
Lodging
Johannesburg has abundant accommodation at all price points. Stay in Sandton, Rosebank, or near FNB Stadium. Major hotel chains and Airbnb widely available. Book early for race weekend as domestic visitors fill nearby options.
Food
Johannesburg has a top-tier restaurant scene. Race-morning nutrition should be pre-prepared. Post-race food available at the FNB Stadium finish area.
Packet Pickup
Multiple registration points at Specialized partner stores (Mon-Fri race week) and final registration at FNB Stadium Dome on Saturday. No race-day registration.
Parking
FNB Stadium precinct has large parking areas. Arrive early on race morning. Follow event signage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at 947 Ride Joburg?

+

Late October is early summer in Johannesburg. Expect 24-27C highs with cool early mornings around 12-15C. The morning start means front-of-pack riders finish before the heat builds, but back markers face warming conditions. The real wildcard is afternoon thunderstorms, which hit Johannesburg frequently in October-November. Rain can turn the last hours miserable for slower riders.

How are the logistics for 947 Ride Joburg?

+

Twenty-eight editions of refinement show. Full road closure across 97km of Johannesburg's busiest streets is a logistical triumph. Seeding waves sort 20,000+ riders by ability. Multiple registration points including Specialized partner stores and the FNB Stadium Dome make pickup convenient. RaceTec chip timing, medical support, and water points are professional-grade. The sheer scale of shutting down the M1 highway and Kyalami circuit for a bicycle race is impressive.

How adventurous is 947 Ride Joburg?

+

Riding on the M1 highway through Johannesburg is an experience you cannot replicate any other day of the year. The Kyalami Grand Prix Circuit adds motorsport heritage to a cycling event. Finishing at FNB Stadium in Soweto connects you to South Africa's cultural heart. For international visitors, the entire experience of racing through Africa's City of Gold is genuinely adventurous.

How prestigious is 947 Ride Joburg?

+

The world's second-largest individually timed cycle race, behind only the Cape Town Cycle Tour. Past winners include Marianne Vos (2014), Daryl Impey (2017), Ryan Gibbons (2024), and Ashleigh Moolman-Pasio (2021). The race has attracted WorldTour talent and is the pinnacle of South African road cycling. Named after Johannesburg's 947 radio station, it carries cultural weight that extends well beyond cycling.

Should I race 947 Ride Joburg?

+

If you want to experience the energy of 20,000+ riders owning the streets of one of Africa's great cities, this race delivers. It is not an extreme endurance test or a technical challenge. It is a celebration of cycling in Johannesburg, and the atmosphere alone makes it worth the entry fee. For international visitors, the favorable exchange rate and the unique experience of riding the M1 highway and Kyalami circuit make it a compelling bucket-list event.
