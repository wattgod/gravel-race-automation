Facts & History

Bend Dirt Fest launched in July 2025 as a new Mudslinger Events production, replacing the cancelled Oregon 24 MTB event on the calendar. Multi-discipline festival—gravel, MTB, trail run—celebrating Bend's outdoor culture. Grew ~120% into its second year (2026). Founded in 2025 (inaugural year). By Mike Ripley / Mudslinger Events (22-year Central Oregon race producer).

Reputation: New (2025-founded) regional festival. Multi-discipline community event.

[03]

The Course

High desert gravel and volcanic cinder roads through Oregon's Deschutes National Forest landscape. Multi-discipline festival course. 54 miles; gain unverified/conflicting across sources (roughly 3,000-4,000 ft).

Signature challenge: High desert heat and sun exposure. Front-loaded climbing in the first 30 miles, descending the final 24.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Bend Dirt Fest?

+

Historical weather data shows average highs of 87°F and lows of 56°F, with a 8% chance of rain on any given day. July courses receive "full sun" with official warnings to "dress for conditions, heat and potential dehydration." Terrain varies between "red hero cinder dirt or fine moon dust" depending on conditions. The high desert sun combined with exposed volcanic terrain demands serious hydration planning and UV protection throughout the day. More weather exposure than Belgian Waffle Ride San Diego.

How are the logistics for Bend Dirt Fest?

+

Athletic Club of Bend for start/finish with designated parking at OSU-Cascades shows solid planning from Mudslinger Events. Grace Perkins notes "I'm surprised (Bend) doesn't have more events like this because everyone does so many different activities. It is fun to have a multisport event where you can have a team and get all the activities going." Mass starts at 8:00 AM cycling, 8:45 AM trail running with established cut-off times demonstrate thoughtful scheduling.

How adventurous is Bend Dirt Fest?

+

Singletrack trails through Deschutes National Forest deliver authentic high desert adventure with volcanic terrain. Mike Ripley designed courses "to create an event that embraced the adventure of Bend, Oregon. Where someone could roll out of bed, go run a half, head over to the Athletic Club, grab their bike — gravel or mountain — and have a great day."

How prestigious is Bend Dirt Fest?

+

Inaugural 2025 event drew Matthew Bird from Adelaide, Australia, who called it "a really good course, with a nice vibe around the event." More than 120 competitors participated in the first year across all disciplines. Organizer Mike Ripley declared "The foundation has been laid for a monster event in 2026" after the successful debut.

Should I race Bend Dirt Fest?

+

This works if you want a legitimate 54-mile dirt challenge without the circus atmosphere of bigger events. The front-loaded early climbing won't break anyone, but it'll remind you why base fitness matters. Skip it if you need competition depth or a longer race history—this is only its second year.
