The Ratings

Course Profile
Logistics 4/5

Slick operation refined over 47 editions. Seeded start waves manage 28,000-35,000 riders with remarkably little chaos. Twelve refreshment stations, medical support, and marshalling are comprehensive. Cape Town International Airport (CPT) is 25-30 minutes from the city center — far easier access than most European mountain events. The Expo at the Cape Town International Convention Centre is well-organized. The only friction: international riders need to navigate South African traffic driving on the left, the sheer scale means packet pickup queues can be substantial, and if connecting through Johannesburg you must collect and re-check bags.

Editorial
Prestige 4/5

The world's largest individually timed cycling event. Running since 1978. Born from a protest ride advocating for cycling infrastructure. The 2026 addition to the UCI Gran Fondo World Series elevates the event from a mass-participation ride to a qualifier for World Championships — a significant prestige boost. Chapman's Peak is iconic in South African culture. The event lacks the deep European cycling mythology of L'Etape or Maratona dles Dolomites, which keeps it from a 5, but within the Southern Hemisphere and the global mass-participation cycling world, this is royalty.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Cape Town Cycle Tour?

+

March in Cape Town is generally benign — warm, dry, sunny, with only two average rain days in the month. The only real hazard is the Southeast wind, and when it blows hard, it dominates the entire experience. In most years, conditions are near-perfect for cycling. The UV is intense but manageable with sunscreen. Compared to Alpine cold, desert heat, or tropical humidity, Cape Town's climate is among the friendliest on the global cycling calendar.

How are the logistics for Cape Town Cycle Tour?

+

Slick operation refined over 47 editions. Seeded start waves manage 28,000-35,000 riders with remarkably little chaos. Twelve refreshment stations, medical support, and marshalling are comprehensive. Cape Town International Airport (CPT) is 25-30 minutes from the city center — far easier access than most European mountain events. The Expo at the Cape Town International Convention Centre is well-organized. The only friction: international riders need to navigate South African traffic driving on the left, the sheer scale means packet pickup queues can be substantial, and if connecting through Johannesburg you must collect and re-check bags.

How adventurous is Cape Town Cycle Tour?

+

This is where the Cape Town Cycle Tour earns its keep. The route circles the entire Cape Peninsula — one of the most dramatic landscapes on Earth. Table Mountain towers over the start. Chapman's Peak Drive is a cliff road carved into the mountainside with the Atlantic Ocean 200m below. You ride past Cape Point, along the Indian Ocean coast, through fishing villages, around the Peninsula's wild southern tip, and finish with the Atlantic Seaboard's Camps Bay and Sea Point. The scenery is genuinely top-tier. There is no cycling event on Earth with a more spectacular urban-coastal route.

How prestigious is Cape Town Cycle Tour?

+

The world's largest individually timed cycling event. Running since 1978. Born from a protest ride advocating for cycling infrastructure. The 2026 addition to the UCI Gran Fondo World Series elevates the event from a mass-participation ride to a qualifier for World Championships — a significant prestige boost. Chapman's Peak is iconic in South African culture. The event lacks the deep European cycling mythology of L'Etape or Maratona dles Dolomites, which keeps it from a 5, but within the Southern Hemisphere and the global mass-participation cycling world, this is royalty.

Should I race Cape Town Cycle Tour?

+

Yes if you want the most spectacular coastal cycling route on Earth and the energy of 28,000-35,000 riders sharing closed roads around the Cape Peninsula. Yes if you want UCI Gran Fondo World Championship qualification in a stunning setting at an unbeatable price point. Skip if you need serious climbing or endurance challenge — 109 km with 1,220m of climbing is moderate. Skip if the Southeast wind gamble (potential event cancellation) is a dealbreaker for a long-haul trip.
