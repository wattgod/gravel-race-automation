RIDERS REPORT
Grassy climb start
Race starts straight up a hill with a grass field that has only one good fast racing line despite being wide. The start is full gas from the gun.
Fire tower
The highest point on the course and a big selective point. This is where a lot starts to happen in the race and the only steep climb. It's where attacks happen and the race breaks up.
Seven Sisters
A punchy, brutal climb that follows Fire Tower Hill. Described as undulations that continue after the main climb.
Power line section
Wide open undulations toward the finish after single track stops. This is where the finish starts to open up.
Course is part mountain bike, part gravel through iconic Wisconsin North Woods following parts of the famous Berky cross country ski trail.
Described as flying through hilly grassy areas where you can smell the forest and earth, especially beautiful in fall when leaves are turning.
Race conditions can include mud when rain clouds are looming.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Chequamegon MTB?

+

Historical weather data shows average highs of 70°F and lows of 54°F, with a 40% chance of rain on any given day. September weather creates equipment gambles, as Hall discovered when a sharp rock punctured his tire during prime racing conditions. Richards called September 'one of the best ever' with perfect temps and moisture, but the month averages 31% chance of rain. When conditions turn, the wide Birkie Trail becomes treacherous enough to cause crashes in the final miles. More weather exposure than Belgian Waffle Ride San Diego.

How are the logistics for Chequamegon MTB?

+

Jenna Rinehart notes this was 'the first time they started the women's field separately from the men,' highlighting how organizers adjust logistics but create new complications. The lottery system requiring March 15 entry and six-month Life Time memberships for priority access remains unnecessarily complex. Point-to-point logistics mean dealing with shuttles between Hayward and Cable, though 43 years of experience shows in race execution.

How adventurous is Chequamegon MTB?

+

The point-to-point route travels through Chequamegon National Forest on trails accessible only race day, creating exclusivity beyond typical loop courses. The course follows the American Birkebeiner Ski Trail through 'fast flowing and wide grassy cuts through the forest' before hitting forest roads and snowmobile routes. Fall conditions and Northwoods terrain provide adventure backdrop, though the wide trail prevents backcountry isolation.

How prestigious is Chequamegon MTB?

+

Elite riders like Vermeulen treat this as a marquee win, with his 2025 victory marking his third Chequamegon title. Rinehart's comment about her '11th Chequamegon race' shows how veterans return year after year. The Life Time Grand Prix Series integration means top cyclists like Rollins and Olshove line up in Cable, while the lottery system exists because demand crushes supply among serious racers.

Should I race Chequamegon MTB?

+

Show up fit or don't show up at all—the Seeley Fire Tower climb doesn't care about your weekend warrior training plan. You need sustained power for constant rollers and the mental fortitude to handle September weather that swings from dust storms to mud festivals. If you can hold 250+ watts for three hours while navigating lottery systems and tourist crowds, this delivers old-school racing credibility.
