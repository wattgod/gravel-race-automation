Self-proclaimed 'Premier Gravel Race of the West' without competitive depth to support the claim.

Facts & History

Chip-timed grassroots gravel event out of Kendrick Park in Sheridan, run by the local bike shop through Bighorn-foothills ranch country. By Dead Swede Hundo LLC / Sheridan Bicycle Company.

Reputation: Small regional Wyoming event.

[03]

The Course

Dead Swede Hundo LLC's chip-timed race from Kendrick Park, Sheridan (~3,750ft — under altitude gate): 100mi 'Crusher' flagship 7,671ft (71% gravel), 60mi 'Creeper' 3,899ft, 40mi 'Cruiser' 2,092ft, 20mi shuttle option from Dayton. Bighorn-foothills tour — punchy climbs, green ranch country, aid ~every 10mi, sweeper vehicle. PRIOR 62mi/3,500ft matched no option — corrected 2026-07-14 (anchor = 100mi Crusher).

Signature challenge: Wyoming high plains wind and open terrain.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Dead Swede Gravel?

+

Historical weather data shows average highs of 79°F and lows of 53°F, with a 41% chance of rain on any given day, and gusts reaching 27 mph. Weather swings wildly year to year. Heidi's 2019 race was blazing hot: 'The still air combined with my black skin suit and hot sun made me want to melt.' Jeffrey's 2022 featured 'amazing weather, low 50s to start, mid 70s in afternoon' but rain dumped 2.5 inches the day before, forcing course changes. More weather exposure than Belgian Waffle Ride San Diego. As Heidi puts it: "Around hour one I started sipping my Tailwind, cognizant of the fact I needed to be taking on the calories and hydration in the sun."

How are the logistics for Dead Swede Gravel?

+

Packet pickup requires day-before arrival only, which Anonymous notes 'pretty much requires that participants book some form of lodging for one night.' Registration via ItsYourRace works fine, and one rider confirms the process 'from registration to packet pickup to post meal collection was seamless.' Still no airport or parking details published. As Heidi puts it: "all done on a 2012 Specialized Crux with canti brakes and relatively narrow tires in comparison to today's trends, and hamburger seat bag. I thumb my nose at you, industry marketing tactics!"

How adventurous is Dead Swede Gravel?

+

The course turns 'north onto the gravel nestled along the base of the Bighorn Mountains' with minimal dust conditions, possibly from road oil treatment or recent rain. One rider loved starting 'at Kendrick Park, right there in the heart of town' with horses 'grazing on the hillside overlooking the park.' Wyoming backcountry through terrain most never see. As Jeffrey Cory puts it: "my passion is bikepacking, and many of those long miles are solo, so the beautiful landscape (see pics) and sky kept me company."

How prestigious is Dead Swede Gravel?

+

Jeffrey finished 64th out of 65 finishers in the 100-mile, spending most of the race dead last with a sweeper truck following him. Heidi notes the mass start crowd was 'mostly filled with people that have never road raced or ridden in a peloton.' Self-proclaimed 'Premier Gravel Race of the West' without competitive depth to support the claim.

Should I race Dead Swede Gravel?

+

Only if you're in Wyoming or seeking a remote high plains experience. Not worth traveling far for.
