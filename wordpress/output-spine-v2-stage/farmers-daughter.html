Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Farmer's Daughter?

+

Historical weather data shows average highs of 70°F and lows of 52°F, with a 43% chance of rain on any given day, and gusts reaching 27 mph. Mid-May weather ranges from challenging to ideal. Cullen MacDonald experienced "hottest days so far this year" with conditions "from outright soup to bright sun," while another rider needed medical attention on "the first hot day of the year in NYC." Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Farmer's Daughter?

+

Start and finish at 182 Hudson Place in Chatham keeps it simple, with motorcycle support providing spare tires throughout the course. Field sizes vary dramatically - sportsfan42069 reported "1200 people over 3 waves" while Cullen MacDonald noted 482 registrants in 2019, suggesting inconsistent capacity management.

How adventurous is Farmer's Daughter?

+

Cullen MacDonald crashed when his "35mm Cross Boss tires right in the gap in between the planks" on a wooden bridge, "which promptly flipped me straight over the bars." The course includes "baby rock gardens link rooty switchbacks to just-rideable mud" but stays within supported farm country.

How prestigious is Farmer's Daughter?

+

Billed as non-competitive but Cullen MacDonald noted it "doesn't feel that way" with the lead group more "aggro racer than we had anticipated." The ambiguous race-versus-ride format and no official finish line or timing chips limit any competitive reputation building.

Should I race Farmer's Daughter?

+

If you're in NY/Northeast and want a local event—yes, it's solid. If you're from out of region—there are better options.
