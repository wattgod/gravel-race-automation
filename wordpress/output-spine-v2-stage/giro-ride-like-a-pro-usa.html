Facts & History

Born from Giro's pro parcours, Ride Like a Pro hit amateurs in 2023 across 6 countries by 2025. USA debut smashed in St. Augustine January 2025 with champ Owen Shott. Relocated to Doral Miami for 2026's sunnier, Italian-flavored winter escape. Founded in 2023. By Giro d'Italia organizers expanding pro routes to amateurs.

Reputation: Rising star for accessible pro vibes. Charity support and Giro branding build cult following fast. Flat-fast formula hooks speed freaks.

The Course

Silky flat loops from Doral through Miami-Dade suburbs scream pure speed. Four timed segments spice endless asphalt with KOM hunts. Minimal elevation lets watts rule—no tactical climbing games. Urban glimpses mix with open-road blasts for pro-peloton feel.

Signature challenge: Aggregate timed segments over full 85 miles—winners forged by consistent power, not one big attack. Flats amplify small gaps; strategy trumps suffering.

RIDERS REPORT
Aim to eat around every 13 minutes during long rides. Start with real foods like brioche with jam in the first half, then move to energy bars and gels when you start suffering in the second half.
Look for 60 to 120 grams of carbs per hour for long rides. The amount varies based on intensity and duration - 30-60 grams per hour for 1-3 hour rides, pushing towards 90 grams per hour for longer or harder efforts.
Practice your fueling strategy in training. Try out any new energy bars or gels during training rides to make sure they agree with your stomach before race day.
