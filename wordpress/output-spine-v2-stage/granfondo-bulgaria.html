TIER 2

Granfondo Bulgaria

Velingrad, Bulgaria · 2026: June 21

VERDICT Granfondo Bulgaria debuts as Bulgaria's first UCI Gran Fondo World Series qualifier, delivering 140km/1905m of Rhodope Mountain suffering around stunning Batak Reservoir. The 21.6km @ 5.4% monster climb (km 78-100) crowns the highest point at 1337m, flanked by long rewarding descents. Velingrad's spa culture and Balkan beauty elevate this beyond typical qualifiers. Strong T2 contender for Eastern European climbers seeking World Champs qualification.

RACE-SPECIFIC: GRANFONDO BULGARIA
HEAT PREP June in Velingrad, Bulgaria can bring heat. Complete 3–4 heat exposure sessions in the final 2 weeks before Granfondo Bulgaria. Increase sodium intake 48 hours before race day.
NUTRITION Target 60–80g carbs/hour for Granfondo Bulgaria’s 87 miles. Start fueling within the first 30 minutes — early fueling prevents late-race collapse. Bonking at mile 60 is a nutrition failure, not a fitness failure.
ALTITUDE Moderate altitude at Granfondo Bulgaria. Arrive 2–3 days early. Reduce intensity expectations by 5–10%. Hydrate aggressively.
TERRAIN Mixed terrain at Granfondo Bulgaria requires surface adaptability. Include weekly rides on mountainous to build confidence. Expect: mountainous, climbs, descents.
