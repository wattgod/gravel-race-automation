Facts & History

Created in response to UCI's formalization of gravel racing, GES was founded 'to provide more of an experience than a competition.' The series launched with 8 events in 2023, expanded to 26 events in 16 countries by 2025, then consolidated to 12 events in 7 countries for 2026. The Traka (Girona) serves as flagship and crown jewel. Founded in 2023. By Gerard and Cristina Freixes (Klassmark, Girona).

Reputation: Europe's premier gravel circuit and the adventure-focused alternative to Life Time's American dominance.

2023: Inaugural season with 8 events
2024: Expansion to 20 events; Mattia De Marchi and Annabel Fisher crowned champions
2025: Peak at 26 events, 16 countries; Rosa Kloser and Magnus Bak Klaris win titles
2026: Consolidation to 12 events; The Rift controversy; calendar conflicts emerge

The Course

A global circuit that delivers genuine adventure across continents. The Traka offers Mediterranean gravel with sea views and Catalan forest trails. Migration throws riders into Kenya's safari lands with wildlife encounters and Maasai villages. Iceland provides volcanic otherworldliness. The Oregon Trail delivers American multi-day stage racing. Each event is distinct, but all share the GES ethos: adventure over pure competition.

Signature challenge: The sheer variety—committing to GES means racing Mediterranean forests, African safari lands, volcanic Iceland, and American mountain ranges. No other series delivers this breadth of adventure.

Race Logistics

Airport
BCN (Barcelona) for The Traka - 30min train to Girona. JKIA (Nairobi) for Migration - concierge service included. Various for other events.
Lodging
Book early for Girona during Traka week—cycling mecca fills up. Kenya packages include Holiday Inn Two Rivers and safari camps. Use Klassmark/Eat Sleep Cycle tour packages for convenience.
Food
Mediterranean cuisine in Spain is excellent. Migration includes meals in packages. Plan for self-sufficiency in more remote events.
Packet Pickup
Varies by event. Pre-race registration typically day before.
Parking
Varies by event. Girona has good city parking; safari events have organized transport.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Gravel Earth Series?

+

Weather significantly impacts racing across the series. The 2023 Ranxo faced "big storms in the area" on Friday, creating mud that made previously easy sections "more technical and added difficulty to the race." Despite muddy conditions, "it was a hot day with temperatures in the 30s Celsius," creating challenging dual conditions of heat and slippery terrain.

How are the logistics for Gravel Earth Series?

+

Global circuit requires significant travel planning. Pete Stetina notes arriving at Ranxo "an hour west of Barcelona" for the series final, where the venue was "El Vilar de la Duquesa, a hotel resort located in the upper part of the medieval village." Most riders stayed at the centralized venue, simplifying race-day logistics despite the international travel complexity.

How adventurous is Gravel Earth Series?

+

This is where Gravel Earth shines. Riding with giraffes and zebras in Kenya's Maasai Mara. Volcanic tectonic rift zones in Iceland. Mediterranean forests with sea views in Girona. 'The hardest and wildest gravel race in the world' (Migration). True bucket-list adventure across multiple continents.

How prestigious is Gravel Earth Series?

+

Europe's premier gravel series and the global alternative to Life Time. The Traka is called 'Europe's Unbound' and the 'crown jewel of European gravel.' Established champions like Magnus Bak Klaris and Rosa Kloser. However, Life Time's deeper pockets and American media dominance mean GES remains second-tier in global consciousness. As Janosch Wintermantel puts it: "His Scott Addict is already good-looking, but said that ahead of the gravel World Championships he will receive a new Scott model, switch to a x1 SRAM front ring, and put some Schwalbe 45 mm G-One RS ..."

Should I race Gravel Earth Series?

+

Yes if you want adventure beyond typical gravel racing and value experience over prize money. Yes if you're European-based or seeking unique global destinations. Yes if community vibe matters more than production polish. Skip if you need significant prize purses, prefer USA-based racing, or can't handle the travel logistics and expenses.
