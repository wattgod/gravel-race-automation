The Ratings

Course Profile
Logistics 2/5

KHS91 notes French gravel events often use MTB trails making them rougher than US equivalents, arriving "a bit broken from the jarring of the course" in previous years. Multiple airport options within 2 hours help, but medical certificate requirements and FFC health module validity periods add bureaucratic complexity for international riders.

Editorial
Prestige 2/5

The 2025 edition hosted the inaugural FFC French Gravel Championships, and 3bikes.fr noted it's becoming "very well known." Trans Continental Race winner Victor Bosoni participated, but the event is still establishing its reputation in the growing French gravel scene rather than commanding international attention.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Gravel Fever?

+

2025 conditions were cold and wet with "no more than 12°C outside" during the day and night temperatures requiring thermal gear. KHS91 appreciated hot soup at checkpoints: "it feels good to eat hot and always the delicious vermicelli soup that every year gives me back so much strength" in the grey, humid conditions. Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Gravel Fever?

+

KHS91 notes French gravel events often use MTB trails making them rougher than US equivalents, arriving "a bit broken from the jarring of the course" in previous years. Multiple airport options within 2 hours help, but medical certificate requirements and FFC health module validity periods add bureaucratic complexity for international riders.

How adventurous is Gravel Fever?

+

The Ultra route passes iconic Loire châteaux including Chambord and Amboise, circles medieval Loches, and traverses the Brenne natural park. However, KHS91's mechanical near Versailles required finding "a bike shop to replace the tire" - suggesting much of the route stays within reach of civilization rather than remote wilderness.

How prestigious is Gravel Fever?

+

The 2025 edition hosted the inaugural FFC French Gravel Championships, and 3bikes.fr noted it's becoming "very well known." Trans Continental Race winner Victor Bosoni participated, but the event is still establishing its reputation in the growing French gravel scene rather than commanding international attention.

Should I race Gravel Fever?

+

If you're in France/Europe and want a local event—yes, it's solid. If you're international—there are better options.
