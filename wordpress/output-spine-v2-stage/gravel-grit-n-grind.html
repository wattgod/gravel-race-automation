The Course

Saturday's UCI GWS qualifier stage from Heagård farm outside Halmstad: 150km (93mi) or 90km option — ~90% gravel across Halland farmlands, forest dual tracks, and rocky descents via Torup/Gräsås/Getinge; GAIN UNPUBLISHED (display Varies). Sunday runs a separate Nordic Gravel Series event (125/90km) in Simlångsdalen — riders can enter either or both days; our plans anchor the SATURDAY UCI stage (single-day registerable, flagship-day precedent). Age-band course split (90km for 60+/50+) reported but LOW-CONFIDENCE — copy must not assert it. Qualifier for 2026 Worlds (Western Australia) + 2027 Euros (Lahti). PRIOR 50mi/2,000ft profile mismatched; 2026 date MOVED to Sep 26 — corrected 2026-07-14.

What is the climate like at Gravel Grit 'n Grind?

+

The race moved from mid-August to late September for 2026 (permit reasons), so older mid-August weather patterns no longer apply to race day.
