Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Gravel Medellín?

+

August racing in Medellín means the "City of Eternal Spring" delivers mild year-round temperatures of 18-28°C thanks to Andean elevation. The timing coincides with Feria de las Flores featuring parades, music, and traditional 'silletas' flower displays throughout the city. Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Gravel Medellín?

+

Route and start location still unconfirmed for a race six months away shows concerning organizational gaps. International bike travel, Colombian customs, and local transportation create multiple failure points. Being inaugural means zero proven systems for packet pickup, parking, or basic race day logistics.

How adventurous is Gravel Medellín?

+

Racing through "coffee-scented mountains of the Western Colombian Andes" with routes passing "through Colombian coffee plantations & taste the best coffee in the world at its source." The setting combines Medellín's transformation from cartel capital to cycling mecca with serious mountain consequence.

How prestigious is Gravel Medellín?

+

New UCI Gravel World Series qualifier organized by the same team behind established UCI events in Brazil, Chile, and Bogotá suggests competent execution. Top 25% qualify for 2026 Worlds in Australia, but being one of 45 global qualifiers dilutes exclusivity factor considerably.

Should I race Gravel Medellín?

+

If you have a big budget and want a Colombian experience—maybe. For most riders—there are better options.
