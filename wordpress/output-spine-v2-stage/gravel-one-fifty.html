Training

RIDERS REPORT
Full bar tire pressure is not good for gravel - riders need to run lower pressure for the sandy conditions.
Going quite a bit wider on tire choice would provide performance gains on this course.
Consider riding with a hydration pack for more water carrying capacity and flexibility between feed stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Gravel One Fifty?

+

Historical weather data shows average highs of 54°F and lows of 40°F, with a 52% chance of rain on any given day, and gusts reaching 29 mph. 2025 edition ran under 'radiant July sun' with 'bright sun and light breeze' creating blistering speeds on hard-packed gravel. James Ion noted stark contrast to previous year's 'mud bath' conditions. July and April dates avoid winter misery but guarantee wind exposure on flat Dutch terrain. Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Gravel One Fifty?

+

Winner Jordan Habets celebrated victory covered in Drenthe dust at the Roden Brink town square finish, with spectators lining crossroads throughout the course ringing cowbells. Netherlands infrastructure delivers solid transport, though no specific airport or accommodation guidance forces European travel planning solo.

How adventurous is Gravel One Fifty?

+

Route connects 'stunning and diverse cycling sites' through mix of dirt roads, gravel roads and grass sections across Drenthe countryside. Course winds through villages in spectator-friendly network of smaller roads and loops, offering pleasant scenery without wilderness challenge or navigation demands.

How prestigious is Gravel One Fifty?

+

UCI Gravel World Series qualifier status drew riders from 26 countries chasing world championship spots, with nearly 2,000 participants in 2025. Pro road racer Jordan Habets used 'road-racing tactics to perfection' to win, but regional European scope limits international bragging rights. As International field puts it: "Riders had registered from 26 countries, all eager to qualify for the UCI Gravel World Championships"

Should I race Gravel One Fifty?

+

If you're in Netherlands/Europe and want a local event—yes, it's solid. If you're international—there are better options.
