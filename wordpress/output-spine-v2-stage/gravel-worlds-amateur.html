RIDERS REPORT
Rolling hills throughout course
87 hills along the 150 mile course with no flat roads in Nebraska. Roller coaster hills that slowly ramp up causing you to lose speed over the top and then having to accelerate down, which zaps your legs.
Deep gravel sections
Many sections of deep gravel that put riders into difficulty. You need to be very relaxed and allow the bike to float, especially if you start slipping and sliding around.
Long straight roads
Plenty of long and straight roads that can wreak havoc if the wind is blowing, though winds were almost non-existent on race day which is rare for this area.
The terrain is more relentless than Unbound according to riders, with constant up and down roller coasters. While technically easier than some races, there are no flat roads in Nebraska.
Roads are in primo shape for much of the course, but deep gravel sections require riders to stay relaxed and let the bike float to avoid slipping and sliding.
About 95% of the course is gravel with significant climbing - over 5,000 feet for the 75-mile course and 11,000 feet for the 150-mile course.

RIDERS REPORT
No outside support allowed - riders must be self-sufficient for flats, mechanical issues, and nutrition. Some choose to carry all water for the day while others use the 2-3 water oases along the course.
Strategy around nutrition and feed zone stops is important since there are only two official checkpoints plus oases and convenience stores for refueling.
One rider planned to carry 6 liters of water to avoid stopping, estimating about 7 hours total race time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Gravel Worlds Amateur?

+

Late August in Nebraska means potential extreme heat conditions, evidenced by early start times and organizer warnings. The 6:30am general field start suggests awareness of heat building across exposed farmland with minimal tree cover during the 15+ hour event window. More weather exposure than Belgian Waffle Ride San Diego.

How are the logistics for Gravel Worlds Amateur?

+

Sandhills Global Event Center provides a proper race headquarters with camping, packet pickup Thursday 10am-7pm, and emergency check-in race morning. Multi-day event structure with expo suggests they understand race weekend logistics. Lincoln offers reasonable access and infrastructure without big-city hassles or parking nightmares.

How adventurous is Gravel Worlds Amateur?

+

Exploring 150 miles through southeastern Nebraska's rural farmlands offers discovery of terrain most riders have never seen. The GW150 includes checkpoints and high-seas refueling opportunities, adding elements beyond typical organized rides while maintaining support structure.

How prestigious is Gravel Worlds Amateur?

+

Year 17 status and the largest single-day gravel prize purse of $150,000 for elites elevates the event profile. Winners receive the distinctive Sword Trophy and title 'Captain of the Gravel Seas' along with World Champions Jersey. Rule #1 is 'BE COOL!' which captures the grassroots-meets-serious-racing atmosphere.

Should I race Gravel Worlds Amateur?

+

If you want a 150-mile ultra-distance challenge with solid organization—yes, it's a legitimate test. If you want terrain variety or pro field depth—look elsewhere.
