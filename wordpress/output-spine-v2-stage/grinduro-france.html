Facts & History

Grinduro France is part of international series. Moved to Vosges in 2024 (new venue). 400-600 riders annually. Founded in 2010s. By Grinduro Series.

Reputation: Festival gravel race. French edition of Grinduro series.

2024: New Vosges venue. Warm, dry (65-75°F). Low DNF (~5%).
[03]

The Course

Enduro format: 4 timed descent stages (technical singletrack), untimed climbs. 62 miles, 6,562 ft. Vosges mountains: steep, forested, technical. Rewards MTB descending, allows social climbing.

Signature challenge: Technical singletrack. MTB skills required. Crashes possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Grinduro France?

+

The 2024 edition started with "sunshine and warm temperatures greeted the riders on Saturday morning," but Paul Errington witnessed conditions change during lunch: "the added drama of a brutal rain storm rolled in, which luckily had had the foresight to ensure the riding portion was done before it arrived." Mountain weather in the Vosges shifts quickly, though timing worked favorably for the inaugural event. Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Grinduro France?

+

The inaugural Vosges event created proper race infrastructure at Lac de Longemer with Paul Errington noting the event village featured "a zone détente, une scène pour le DJ et les concerts, un bar, les stands des partenaires (Shimano, WishOne, Odlo, etc.), et une zone de lavage pour les vélos." Getting to Xonrupt-Longemer still requires commitment beyond booking a Paris flight, but the base camp organization handled 200 riders smoothly with clear packet pickup and sponsor integration.

How adventurous is Grinduro France?

+

Julien Sommier described traversing "la Réserve Naturelle Nationale du Frankenthal-Missheimle et la Réserve Biologique Dirigée de la Haute-Meurthe" where riders "avons traversé des forêts magnifiques, longé des rivières, et profité de panoramas grandioses sur le massif vosgien." The varied terrain features everything from technical singletrack through protected forests to fast double track descents, rewarding exploration beyond pure speed.

How prestigious is Grinduro France?

+

The inaugural European Grinduro attracted notable riders including "downhill legend Wyn Masters" who "drops in and cleans the first section easily" on Stage 3's technical singletrack. The brand's California origins and global following created anticipation, with James Deane noting the Vosges "possède tous les ingrédients pour faire du Grinduro France, un événement parfait."

Should I race Grinduro France?

+

If you can handle 100 kilometers of varied terrain without crying about gravel bike handling, this works. The 2,000 meters of climbing spread across French forest roads won't destroy you, but the mix of surfaces rewards riders who can adapt rather than specialists. Show up for the weekend festival vibe, not pure racing—Grinduro's DNA is equal parts suffering and beer.
