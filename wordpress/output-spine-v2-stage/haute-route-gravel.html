Logistics 2/5

Multi-day Alpine stage racing requires complex coordination, but actual rider experiences show gaps. RIDERS REPORT
Jebel Akhdar MI 19
19 kilometers at 8% average gradient with 4 kilometers of 10.5%. Described as the most difficult climb ever done by the rider with seriously steep ramps hitting 20% in the first 10 meters. Unrelenting climb with extreme heat adding to the challenge.
Col du Izoard
The infamous climb that brought one rider to nearly giving up, described as one of the toughest tests on the route requiring support from fellow riders to complete.
22km climb to 2200 meters
Long 22 kilometer climb from 1500 to 2200 meters elevation where power numbers drastically decrease due to thin air and less oxygen availability compared to sea level.
Climbs feature seriously steep ramps with gradients hitting 20% in opening sections, making gear selection immediately critical.
High altitude sections above 1500 meters significantly impact power output due to thin air and reduced oxygen availability.

RIDERS REPORT
On extreme climbs, pace yourself and ride to power rather than trying to stay with faster riders early on the climb.
Nutrition is critical - big riders need to consume 800-1300 grams of carbs daily totaling 6000-8000 calories to maintain performance across multiple stages.
When struggling mentally on climbs, fellow riders can provide crucial support - accept help and consume nutrition even when you don't want to.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Haute Route Gravel?

+

High Alpine conditions create physiological demands beyond weather. Mark Gilmour had to adjust his refueling strategy after cramping: 'I wasn't replenishing the salts I was losing through sweating and the effort' and added more salt to prevent further issues. The multi-day exposure at altitude requires constant attention to hydration and electrolyte management across varying mountain weather conditions. Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Haute Route Gravel?

+

Multi-day Alpine stage racing requires complex coordination, but actual rider experiences show gaps. Maxime Cauchois needed to pre-order nutrition from Gelvio before the event, suggesting limited on-site support. Mark Gilmour noted strict daily time cuts that create DNF pressure, while riders report that official accommodation packages deliver 'acceptable, but barely' hotels with 'sub-par' food according to multiple participants.

How adventurous is Haute Route Gravel?

+

Routes traverse historic Alpine passes steeped in cycling and cultural history. Rupert Guinness described it as 'a ride back in time' through 'mountains steeped in cycling history' where 'passes we tackled read like a brutal week at the Tour de France.' The adventure extends beyond cycling to experiencing the same legendary climbs used in professional racing, following ancient trade routes through iconic European mountain terrain.

How prestigious is Haute Route Gravel?

+

The Haute Route brand built reputation on road events, expanding into gravel through the acquired Gravel Epic series. Created in 2019 and backed by IRONMAN Group since 2021, it positions itself as 'unprecedented in the cycling and gravel events industry' but lacks the established gravel racing credibility of purpose-built events. As Official description puts it: "Since its beginning in 2011, the single event has expanded to a series of races across the world designed to give riders a challenging, immersive, and premium experience through the world's most iconi..."

Should I race Haute Route Gravel?

+

If you have a large budget and want a supported Alpine multi-stage experience—maybe. For most riders—there are better value options.
