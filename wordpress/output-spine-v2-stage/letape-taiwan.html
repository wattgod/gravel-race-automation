Facts & History

Launched 2024 as Asia's first L'Etape, transforming Sun Moon Lake's famed paths into TdF-certified haven. Blends Taiwanese tourism push with global cycling prestige. Second edition 2025 drew 2,600, cementing its rise. Founded in 2024.

Reputation: Rapid riser as top-tier destination. TdF polish meets exotic beauty—buzz builds fast.

2025: 2,600 riders conquer in pro style
KOM Challenge debuts with 12% walls
Freshly paved 147 road elevates routes
Global clubs flock for Asian TdF debut

The Course

Starts fast downhill from Xiangshan into Puli flats, then unleashes KOM beast on Road 147. Winding lake returns via Road 21 grind steady pain amid emerald bliss. Pro closed roads amplify Tour intensity.

Signature challenge: The Road 147 KOM: 7km of escalating pain peaking at 12%, freshly paved for max suffering. Tests resolve amid rice fields—true TdF suffering in Taiwan.

Race Logistics

Airport
Fly into Taichung (RMQ) 1hr away or Taipei (TPE) 3hrs—shuttles abound. Trains to Nantou seamless for bike bags.
Lodging
Lake resorts book early—Xiangshan area prime. Mix camping for budget or luxe spas post-ride.
Food
Night markets fuel carb feasts—stinky tofu to beef noodles. Event aid handles race day perfectly.
Packet Pickup
Xiangshan Visitor Center day prior—smooth pro setup.
Parking
Ample lakeside lots for event—car-free roads rule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at L'Etape Taiwan by Tour de France?

+

Spring warmth teases tropical sweat-fests, humidity bites legs late. Breezes save the day amid peaks. Pack chamois cream—Taiwan heat builds character.

How are the logistics for L'Etape Taiwan by Tour de France?

+

Taiwan's infrastructure shines—near Taichung hubs make access seamless. Closed roads scream pro event. Only gripe: international flights demand planning, but lake lodges seal the deal.

How adventurous is L'Etape Taiwan by Tour de France?

+

Exotic Taiwan lake, rice paddies, temples—pure escapism. TdF flair in Asia flips the script on Euro snoozefests. You'll Instagram harder than you pedal.

How prestigious is L'Etape Taiwan by Tour de France?

+

Official Tour de France L'Etape badge is gold—wear that jersey with pride. Global series elevates this beyond local grind. Instant cred for any palmares.

Should I race L'Etape Taiwan by Tour de France?

+

Yes if craving prestige jersey and Asian vibes with manageable suffering. Skip if mega-elevation junkie or hate humidity.
