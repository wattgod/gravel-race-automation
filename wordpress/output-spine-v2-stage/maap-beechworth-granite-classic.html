MAAP-sponsored gravel event showcasing Victorian high country terrain in historic Beechworth. By MAAP (cycling apparel brand).

RIDERS REPORT
Sandy sections
Riders report full sandy bogs and squirrly conditions that can cause crashes. One rider had a decent crash in sand up to their shins before the last rest stop, losing their wheel while going decently fast.
Tree roots
Riders mention hitting tree roots that can cause crashes and injuries.
The gravel sections are described as skatey when dry, with some sections being real squirly areas but totally manageable and doable. There are technical bits including full sandy bogs and various squirrly conditions throughout the farmlands.
Course features technical terrain that riders need to scout beforehand to identify climbs and challenging sections.

RIDERS REPORT
For nutrition, aim for about 60 to 80 grams of carbohydrate per hour, which equates to one hydration bottle with carbohydrate fuel and at least one packet of shot blocks, varying based on how you feel during the ride since sometimes you don't want that much sweet stuff in your stomach.
It's important to check out where the feed stations are, particularly if you're relying on the event organization for your fueling.
Reconning the course beforehand helps you acclimatize to the terrain and identify where the climbs are, plus ensures you don't get lost at the start and finish areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at MAAP Beechworth Granite Classic?

+

Historical weather data shows average highs of 64°F and lows of 49°F, with a 43% chance of rain on any given day. Joshua Tang from Singapore tried "to keep warm as I waited in anticipation at the start line" before conditions improved as "the sun came out." April weather in Victoria's High Country means shoulder season gambles where Mount Stanley's red-clay becomes unrideable when wet, adding unpredictability to what could be perfect autumn riding. More weather exposure than Belgian Waffle Ride San Diego.

How are the logistics for MAAP Beechworth Granite Classic?

+

Lewis Ciddor notes organizers "have done such a great job of fostering a really inclusive and welcoming atmosphere" with hot quesadillas, coffee, and donuts replacing typical gel tables. The 2024 food shortage issue prompted expanded feed stations for 2025, but with 1,300 riders registered and max capacity at 1,000, accommodation in regional Beechworth books fast.

How adventurous is MAAP Beechworth Granite Classic?

+

Riders traverse "stunning private property in Byawatha" with "champagne gravel sections" and grassy breaks through farmland. Hunter Blair reports "stories of near-misses with Kangaroos" and notes how "dust and sweat covered the faces of all competitors - like warpaint uniting the fans of their favourite sports team."

How prestigious is MAAP Beechworth Granite Classic?

+

MAAP ultra-endurance rider Lewis Ciddor's participation signals growing credibility among serious athletes, but organizer Ben Kraus explicitly calls this "anything but a competition" with no podiums. The event attracts 1,300 riders seeking dirt roads over Instagram posts, building reputation through inclusive atmosphere rather than elite recognition.

Should I race MAAP Beechworth Granite Classic?

+

If you're in Australia—yes, it's a well-organized event with excellent terrain. If you're international—probably not worth the travel unless you're combining with Australian tourism or other races.
