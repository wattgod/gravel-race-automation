The Course

1,215 km loop from Umeå across Scandinavian peninsula to Norwegian Sea fjords/Mo i Rana and back. 13 controls: Lycksele (122 km), Storuman, Mo i Rana (546 km), Arctic Circle Centre (643 km), Tjaktjaure (722 km), Åmsele (1084 km). Asphalt via river valleys/lakes/fjords; snow-capped climbs to 647 m; 158 km remote stage. **Suffering Zones:** - Early border climb (headwinds/cold); - 158 km monotonous remote; - Norwegian mountains (snow/rain/narrow frost-damaged roads); - Arctic Circle push (722 km isolation).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Midnight Sun Randonnée?

+

Held in mid-June amid the midnight sun, with continuous daylight from Umeå to Mo i Rana (June 9-July 4), offering ideal long northern summer conditions for non-stop riding in wild Scandinavian nature[3].

How are the logistics for Midnight Sun Randonnée?

+

The Midnight Sun Randonnée offers solid self-guided logistics with 11 controls, suggested accommodations along the 1213 km loop from Brännland Inn near Umeå to Scandic Plaza in Umeå via Mo i Rana and Norwegian Sea fjords, and packages including start/finish stays, though participants must book intermediate nights independently[1][2][3][4].

How adventurous is Midnight Sun Randonnée?

+

This self-supported epic traverses remote forests, wild rivers in UNESCO reserves, over the Arctic Circle to Norwegian Sea fjords, with minimal traffic and potential for unforeseen events in sparsely populated areas, maximizing raw adventure[1][2][3].

How prestigious is Midnight Sun Randonnée?

+

As an annual randonnée attracting international cyclists along the established Midnight Sun Route, it holds regional recognition with finisher awards like special sweaters for 1000+ km, but lacks global ultra-distance prestige[3][5].
