Course Profile
Logistics 2/5

The free community campout starts at 10am behind Wabeno Logging Museum, capped at 15 riders who must be willing to ford a 'slow moving and waist deep river' and camp without facilities. Registration filled up quickly in 2025 with a waitlist starting March 19th, but basic details like parking and packet pickup remain unspecified since there's nothing to pick up.

Editorial
Prestige 1/5

This is a free community campout capped at 15 riders, designed to 'build a stronger bikepacking community by learning from each other.' The organizer emphasizes it's 'for all levels of bikepackers, veterans and those interested in being introduced to bikepacking with an emphasis on adventure' - educational rather than competitive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Nicolet Hot Days?

+

Mid-August timing lives up to the 'Hot Days' name. Shane Hitz reported 'The heat was cranking so I did use almost all of the four liters I was carrying on one section' during a June attempt, noting the area 'hasn't had meaningful rain since the beginning of May so in a normal year the sand would be a bit tamer.' Expect heat, humidity, and potentially loose conditions. More weather exposure than Belgian Waffle Ride San Diego.

How are the logistics for Nicolet Hot Days?

+

The free community campout starts at 10am behind Wabeno Logging Museum, capped at 15 riders who must be willing to ford a 'slow moving and waist deep river' and camp without facilities. Registration filled up quickly in 2025 with a waitlist starting March 19th, but basic details like parking and packet pickup remain unspecified since there's nothing to pick up.

How adventurous is Nicolet Hot Days?

+

The route requires fording a waist-deep river and camping at remote National Forest spots without facilities. Shane Hitz notes 'Camping in dispersed spots in the National Forest is always a magical experience when doing stuff like this,' while the organizer warns participants must be comfortable with no outhouse and willing to ride two-tracks through isolated terrain.

How prestigious is Nicolet Hot Days?

+

This is a free community campout capped at 15 riders, designed to 'build a stronger bikepacking community by learning from each other.' The organizer emphasizes it's 'for all levels of bikepackers, veterans and those interested in being introduced to bikepacking with an emphasis on adventure' - educational rather than competitive.

Should I race Nicolet Hot Days?

+

If you're local to Wisconsin—yes, it's good value. If you're traveling—skip it.
