The Ratings

Course Profile
Logistics 4/5

Thirty-four editions of organizational refinement by Pena Ciclista Edelweiss, backed by over 1,000 volunteers. Six aid stations, medical support, broom wagon, full road closures across both Spain and France — coordinating an international border crossing for 11,000 cyclists is genuinely impressive. The lottery system manages demand fairly (though the EUR4 non-refundable fee stings if you lose). The only knock: Sabinanigo is a small Pyrenean town requiring 1.5-2.5 hour transfers from Zaragoza or Pau airports.

Editorial
Prestige 5/5

Spain's most famous sportive, period. Running since 1991, inspired by La Marmotte, and now standing as its Pyrenean equal. The lottery system exists because 20,000+ riders chase 9,000 spots. Professional cyclists — Miguel Indurain, Alejandro Valverde, Haimar Zubeldia, Luis Leon Sanchez, Aleix Espargaro — ride alongside amateurs. The event is a UCI Gran Fondo qualifier. In Spain, saying 'I did the Quebrantahuesos' carries the same weight as saying 'I did La Marmotte' in France.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Marcha Cicloturista Quebrantahuesos?

+

Mid-June Pyrenees is generally favorable — warm valleys, cooler summits, long daylight hours. The temperature range is less extreme than the high Alps (no 2,600m summits here). However, Pyrenean weather is notoriously fickle. The 2025 edition was marked by significant heat, which can be devastating on the exposed 28 km Portalet climb. Wind on the high passes is the other factor that can turn a good day bad.

How are the logistics for Marcha Cicloturista Quebrantahuesos?

+

Thirty-four editions of organizational refinement by Pena Ciclista Edelweiss, backed by over 1,000 volunteers. Six aid stations, medical support, broom wagon, full road closures across both Spain and France — coordinating an international border crossing for 11,000 cyclists is genuinely impressive. The lottery system manages demand fairly (though the EUR4 non-refundable fee stings if you lose). The only knock: Sabinanigo is a small Pyrenean town requiring 1.5-2.5 hour transfers from Zaragoza or Pau airports.

How adventurous is Marcha Cicloturista Quebrantahuesos?

+

The route crosses an international border — departing Spain via the Somport, winding through the French Bearnais valleys and the Ossau Valley beneath the Pic du Midi d'Ossau, then returning to Spain over the Portalet. This is bearded vulture territory: the lammergeier (quebrantahuesos) soars above the same cols you're climbing. The Pyrenean landscape — dramatic limestone peaks, glacial valleys, medieval villages — is genuinely spectacular and wilder than the Alps.

How prestigious is Marcha Cicloturista Quebrantahuesos?

+

Spain's most famous sportive, period. Running since 1991, inspired by La Marmotte, and now standing as its Pyrenean equal. The lottery system exists because 20,000+ riders chase 9,000 spots. Professional cyclists — Miguel Indurain, Alejandro Valverde, Haimar Zubeldia, Luis Leon Sanchez, Aleix Espargaro — ride alongside amateurs. The event is a UCI Gran Fondo qualifier. In Spain, saying 'I did the Quebrantahuesos' carries the same weight as saying 'I did La Marmotte' in France.

Should I race Marcha Cicloturista Quebrantahuesos?

+

Yes if you want the definitive Pyrenean cycling experience and can sustain 7-12 hours of mountain climbing. Yes if you want to ride the same cols as Indurain and Valverde alongside 9,000 other cyclists on fully closed roads. Yes if you've done La Marmotte and want its Pyrenean counterpart. Skip if lottery frustration will ruin your planning (backup options exist through tour operators). Skip if you need technical off-road terrain — this is pure road climbing.
