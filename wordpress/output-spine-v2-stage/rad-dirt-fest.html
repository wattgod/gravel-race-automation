Facts & History

Rad Dirt Fest founded ~2018. Salida, CO. 100 km, 500-800 riders. Colorado Rockies. High altitude gravel. Founded in ~2018. By Life Time Events.

Reputation: Colorado gravel scene. Salida. High altitude. Technical climbs. Well-organized.

2023: Cool, dry (12-22°C)
2024: Variable, some rain (10-25°C)

The Course

100 km, 2,500 m. 80% gravel, 20% dirt. Colorado Rockies—high altitude (2,200-3,000 m), technical climbs. Technical rating: 4/5.

Signature challenge: 100 km + 2,500 m elevation + high altitude (2,200-3,000 m). Colorado Rockies.

RIDERS REPORT
20% grade opening climb
Race starts with a big punchy climb that's 20 plus percent grade. Sets up visibility for the peloton and creates early selection.
Final left turn onto Main Street
After 113 miles of racing, the only thing standing between riders and the finish line is the last left hand turn onto Main Street.
Highway section with no shoulder
Rough stretch of highway with 65 mph traffic and no shoulder for a section that riders found unenjoyable and potentially dangerous.
Deep rock gravel section
Miles of deep rock gravel surface that riders describe as not awesome to ride on.
Road surfaces get rougher and stickier when wet, turning more clay-like. Deep rock gravel sections are challenging to ride on for extended miles.
Mix of flat plains riding with long views followed by climbing into the hills toward the Spanish Peaks. Transition from plains to mountains provides varied terrain.

STRUCTURE 15min warm-up → 2 sets of 3×(20sec surge @ 130% FTP + 90sec settle @ 85% FTP) / 5min between sets → cool-down
EXECUTION Out of the saddle for every surge — explosive, violent power. Then immediately drop to tempo and SETTLE. Smooth your breathing. Recover while still working. This is what gravel racing IS: attack the rock garden, settle on the straight, attack the climb, settle on the descent. Over and over.
POWER Surges: 125–135% FTP | Settle: 83–87% FTP
CADENCE 100–120rpm surge (explosive) | 85–90rpm settle (smooth)
POSITION Standing for surges | Seated in drops for settle
RPE Surges: 9–10 | Settle: 5–6
RACE-SPECIFIC: RAD DIRT FEST
NUTRITION Target 40–60g carbs/hour for Rad Dirt Fest’s 62 miles. Front-load calories in the first half. One bottle per hour minimum, more in heat.
ALTITUDE Rad Dirt Fest with 8,202ft of climbing, much of it above 8,000ft demands altitude preparation. Arrive 5–7 days early for acclimatization. Expect 5–15% power reduction at altitude. Increase iron intake 4 weeks out. Hydrate aggressively — altitude increases fluid loss by 20–40%. High altitude (2,200-3,000 m); Thin air.
TERRAIN Highly technical terrain at Rad Dirt Fest demands practice on similar surfaces. Ride colorado rockies—high altitude technical terrain at race-day cadence weekly. Expect: colorado rockies gravel (80%), dirt roads (20%), high altitude (2,200-3,000 m). Practice cornering, descending, and power delivery on unstable surfaces. Dial in tire pressure before race week — 5 PSI wrong costs you 15+ minutes over 62 miles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Rad Dirt Fest?

+

Late September offers 73-80°F highs with 51°F lows, but that high desert environment creates hydration challenges - expect 'chapped lips, dry throat, empty bottles.' 72% sunshine sounds nice until you're exposed for hours. Wind averages 14.7 mph, which becomes a factor on those open mesas. Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL. As Hannah puts it: "Even though the weather was looking questionable, I was able to finish my first 100 mile ride and beat the rain. Trinidad gravel lived up to all the hype and my legs felt strong throughout the ride."

How are the logistics for Rad Dirt Fest?

+

Life Time's corporate machine delivers professional organization, but that 31-mile stretch with no cell service between aid stations creates anxiety for many riders. Three crew access points help, but Trinidad's remote location means limited backup options. Spanish Peaks Inn checkpoint at mile 42.8 provides key support, assuming you make the 1:20 PM cutoff.

How adventurous is Rad Dirt Fest?

+

4,775 square miles of Las Animas County gravel roads deliver exploration through Colorado's largest county. Old railroad ruins, dilapidated mining history, and transition from high desert to alpine forests create real discovery. That no-service 31-mile section forces self-reliance that most corporate events avoid.

How prestigious is Rad Dirt Fest?

+

Life Time owns this race, which means corporate polish but zero grassroots soul. Keegan Swenson showed up once in 2024 and won by 83 seconds over Stetina, but it's still fighting for relevance against UNBOUND. The Grand Prix inclusion gives it credibility, but getting dropped from 2025 then added back for 2026 screams second-tier status.

Should I race Rad Dirt Fest?

+

If you can hold steady watts for five hours at altitude without external validation, this is your race. The gravel is buttery smooth, but 8,900 feet doesn't negotiate with your sea-level fitness. Show up undertrained and that remote backcountry section becomes a very expensive therapy session.
