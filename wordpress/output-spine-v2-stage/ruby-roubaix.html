Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Ruby Roubaix?

+

Late June conditions range from 40-60°F starts to afternoon highs in the 80s-90s, with typical SSW winds that 'can be any direction.' Organizers note the weather variability: 'It can be hotter, and it is not unknown to be snowing on the summer solstice.' Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Ruby Roubaix?

+

Packet pickup directions remain 'find the schoolhouse on race day.' No airport info, vague lodging suggestions to 'check out Elko,' zero parking details. The research data still reads like someone forgot to finish building the website.

How adventurous is Ruby Roubaix?

+

Routes traverse the Ruby Lake National Wildlife Refuge, Lee, Jiggs, and Ruby Valley on mixed terrain through spectacular Ruby Mountains backcountry. Potential water crossings 'depending on winter snowpack and spring storms' add variability to the remote northern Nevada adventure that marketing grants aim to 'draw them off I-80.'

How prestigious is Ruby Roubaix?

+

Sixth-year Rotary Club fundraiser founded by retired pediatrician Dr. Kevin Dinwiddie, modeled after Reno's former 'Edible Pedal' event. The 2023 field of 165 registered riders reflects regional appeal rather than national recognition, with proceeds funding youth programs and scholarships.

Should I race Ruby Roubaix?

+

Only if you're in Nevada or seeking extreme remote wilderness. Not worth the long drive for the race alone.
