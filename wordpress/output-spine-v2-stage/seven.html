Facts & History

Created to showcase Western Australia's incredible gravel terrain through the 'Golden Triangle' of forestry. Quickly became the largest gravel race in the Southern Hemisphere and earned UCI Gravel World Series status. Founded in 2019. By SEVEN Gravel Race team.

Reputation: The World Championship host—where Australian wilderness meets elite gravel racing.

Secured 2026 UCI Gravel World Championships hosting
Grew to 1,500+ riders—largest in Southern Hemisphere
Part of UCI Gravel World Series qualifier system
[03]

The Course

A relentless climbing race through Western Australian pine plantations and state forests. The 'parallel singletrack' roads are often just two tire tracks wide with grass in the middle. Sharp quartz grit and mica create constant flat tire risk. 12-13 categorized climbs with 3,200m total elevation in 125km—one of the highest climbing ratios in gravel racing.

Signature challenge: The Crucible—the 20%+ grade climb on loose surface where 'I went to hell and back... gradients frequently touching 20%' is the common refrain.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Seven?

+

Australian autumn means cool 45°F mornings warming to pleasant 70°F days—no outback furnace here. May marks the start of wet season with 600mm annual rainfall, so rain can turn clay sections slick and make descents sketchy. It's actually civilized weather, which is probably why this race attracts 1,500 masochists. Similar climate challenges to SBT GRVL.

How are the logistics for Seven?

+

Fly into Perth, drive 3 hours south to a town of 1,300 people where accommodation sells out instantly. You'll need a rental car because public transport to Nannup doesn't exist for cyclists with bike boxes. The saving grace is camping at the event village, which is the authentic way anyway, and Margaret River is only 45 minutes if you need actual beds.

How adventurous is Seven?

+

The official survival guide warns about kangaroo, emu, and wild pig collisions on silent descents through active logging areas with zero cell signal. You're reliant on moto marshals in wilderness where wildlife can end your race violently. This is proper adventure cycling with real consequences, not manicured gravel theater.

How prestigious is Seven?

+

UCI Gravel World Series qualifier and HOST of 2026 UCI Gravel World Championships. Largest gravel race in the Southern Hemisphere. The event that put Australian gravel on the global map.

Should I race Seven?

+

Yes if you want a bucket-list Australian adventure and handle technical terrain. Yes if you're chasing UCI Gravel World Series. Skip if the travel commitment to Western Australia is prohibitive.
