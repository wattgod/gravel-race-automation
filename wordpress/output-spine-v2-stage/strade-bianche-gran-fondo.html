Facts & History

Created in 2015 as the amateur companion to the WorldTour Strade Bianche (pro race est. 2007). Allows amateur riders to tackle the same iconic white gravel roads and finish in Piazza del Campo. Grew to 8,500 riders from 50+ countries by the 11th edition in 2026. Founded in 2016. By RCS Sport / SSD RCS Active Team.

Reputation: The cycling pilgrimage—where fans ride the roads of their heroes.

Same finish as WorldTour race—Piazza del Campo
Route varies slightly but includes signature sectors
Sells out quickly each September
[03]

The Course

A mini spring classic through Tuscan countryside. The strade bianche sectors are the stars—compacted clay roads that become dusty highways when dry and grey paste when wet. Between sectors, fast paved roads with constant punchy climbs. The finish on Via Santa Caterina into Piazza del Campo is cycling theater at its finest.

Signature challenge: Via Santa Caterina—the 16% cobbled finish into Piazza del Campo where you either crush it or walk in front of thousands.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Gran Fondo Strade Bianche?

+

March in Tuscany gives you proper cycling weather - 14-16°C highs with about a 24% chance of rain on any given day. You'll need layers for the morning chill but won't be suffering in summer heat when the dust gets brutal. The humidity sits around 75%, which keeps things comfortable when you're grinding through those gravel sectors. Milder conditions than Crusher in the Tushar. As Anonymous rider puts it: "Heat and dust — It is a very, very demanding race. The first time I struggled with cold and mud, this time with heat and dust."

How are the logistics for Gran Fondo Strade Bianche?

+

RCS Sport knows how to run a race - they've got the pro Strade Bianche dialed and the gran fondo benefits from that expertise. Registration opens in September and sells out the 8,500-10,000 spots quickly, which tells you everything about demand versus supply. Starting at Fortezza Medicea and finishing in Piazza del Campo gives you proper start/finish infrastructure, not some parking lot setup. As Final climb to Siena puts it: "The final climb in Siena, on the narrow street of Via Santa Caterina, peaks at 16 per cent, ahead of the finish in the city's cavernous square, the Piazza del Campo."

How adventurous is Gran Fondo Strade Bianche?

+

You're riding the same roads as Pogačar and the World Tour peloton, finishing in one of Italy's most iconic piazzas the day after the pros tear it up. The white gravel roads through Chianti vineyards aren't just scenic tourism - they're cycling history dating back to L'Eroica in 1997. Racing through sectors named after Fabian Cancellara while 5,000 other riders spread across Tuscany's back roads delivers the full European gran fondo experience.

How prestigious is Gran Fondo Strade Bianche?

+

The amateur version of one of cycling's most beautiful WorldTour races. Riding the strade bianche sectors that Pogacar and van der Poel attack on is bucket-list stuff. Cycling heritage is unmatched.

Should I race Gran Fondo Strade Bianche?

+

Yes if you love cycling culture and want the iconic experience. Yes if you can handle March weather variability. Skip if you want pure gravel racing—this is Italian road cycling with gravel sectors.
