Frequently Asked Questions

What is the climate like at Unravel Gravel (VA State Championship)?

+

Historical weather data shows average highs of 80°F and lows of 62°F, with a 39% chance of rain on any given day. October racing in Virginia delivered a 21% DNF rate in 2024, with 16 riders unable to complete the 100K distance. Multiple DNFs occurred after lap one completion, suggesting conditions or accumulated fatigue created finishing challenges. The 50K distance showed significantly lower dropout rates, pointing to distance-weather interaction effects. More weather exposure than Belgian Waffle Ride San Diego.

How are the logistics for Unravel Gravel (VA State Championship)?

+

The race has bounced between Richmond, Cumberland, and Harrisonburg over recent years, with 2025 venue still TBA according to organizers. Registration caps at 200 riders, but basic details like start times and aid stations remain unpublished.

How adventurous is Unravel Gravel (VA State Championship)?

+

The course combines fire road grinding with what organizers call 'noodle-bar friendly single track' through Bear Creek Lake State Park. Two-lap format means riders preview technical sections before racing them at full intensity. The 21% DNF rate suggests the terrain delivers on adventure promises without becoming unrideable for most participants.

How prestigious is Unravel Gravel (VA State Championship)?

+

USAC Virginia State Championship status attracts regional elite talent like Presley Evans and Andrea Buttine, but field size remains modest at 75 starters for the main event. Teams like Miller School of Albemarle and Fount Cycling Guild participate, indicating solid regional recognition within Virginia's gravel racing scene.

Should I race Unravel Gravel (VA State Championship)?

+

If you're local to VA/Mid-Atlantic—yes, it's solid. If you're traveling far—skip it.
