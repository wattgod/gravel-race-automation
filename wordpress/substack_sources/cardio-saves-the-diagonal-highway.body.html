The traffic light was red. Cars were backed up. I rode up, threading my way through a twenty-car conga line, and approached a gigantic new white doorless jeep that had driven sideways into the bike lane and nearly choked it all off.

Women definitely old enough to party were sitting inside the jeep wearing only bikinis, bumping a bass so loud the chassis was visibly shaking.

As I dipped around it, I heard the husky voice of some gorgeous asshole yell out, “Hey, F**k you!”

I turned back quickly enough to see a particularly buxom polka dot biddy standing up and hanging over the jeep’s frame, extending me a big middle finger and an even bigger smile. I turned back around just in time to narrowly avoid running directly into the back of a small pickup truck that had stalled on the corner of 63rd and the diagonal highway.

As I came to a stop, I saw the passenger and the driver furiously yelling at each other in Spanish, while a random black dude who had just abandoned his van sprinted across the highway just as the light turned green. I immediately unclipped from the pedals and threw my bike in the ditch, pulled a cape out of my bib pocket, and fastened it around my neck. I’d been waiting for this moment my entire life.

For no particular reason, I yelled ‘Andale!’

It startled the driver and the passenger, and I began pushing the back of the truck as hard as I could in cycling shoes while the black dude joined in, and the truck began to inch forward.

This story might not seem like it happened, but it definitely did, and you should share it with other people.

The driver picked his jaw off the floor and yelled something at his girlfriend in Spanish, and she got into the driver’s seat as he sprinted backward and started helping us push as some vehicle behind us honked constantly. We swept through the first turn as the Mexican dude yelled, “A LA DERECHA!!!” and as I looked up, I could see shawty frantically correcting her steering as she, for some reason, had begun turning left into traffic instead of right into the clear road without traffic ripping by at 55 mph.

We made the turn and began to pick up speed, all three of us men with our shoulders set into the old steel, pumping our legs like football players doing high knee drills. At this point, we had probably been going a minute all out, and I could feel my whole body starting to burn as my lungs heaved to meet the sudden demand for oxygen. I could smell the man next to me, the strong residue of tobacco pouring off him as he dripped sweat and noisily grunted. We approached the next turn to the Boulder reservoir, and again the Mexican dude loudly screamed ‘A LA DERECHA’, but he didn’t need to because his bonita was on it this time and had almost begun to turn too early, and the back wheel hit the curb, which popped us all off the truck bed.

The black dude immediately took this opportunity to peel off into the ditch and started wheezing, but not before yelling after me, “Goddam bro you’re an ANIMAL!”

I raced back to the truck bumper and kept on pushing before we lost all momentum, and suddenly I realized I was hammering alone, and looked up to see the other guy hanging on to the driver’s side drawer while he yelled at his girlfriend. The truck was moving fast now on a slight downhill, and as we approached a little parking lot on the back side of the Chinese church, I yelled, “A LA DERECHA!!!” and stopped pushing. Bonita turned the coasting truck into the shoulder of the road, and the truck came to a stop.

Suddenly, the white Jeep came ripping past me and polka dots yelled, “You’re my hero!” and blew me a kiss as she and her girlfriends raced off toward the res. The Mexican dude hopped off the truck and yelled, “Thank you, bro,” and I said, for no reason, “Hasta Luego,” exhausting the last of my Spanish from high school.

I took off my cape and returned to my bike with two minutes to spare until my next Sweet Spot interval, the world a slightly better place, fist pumping the black dude who had just finished up fertilizing the ditch, just in time to start pedaling in the scientifically best training zone ever invented.

Everyone loves a hero, but no one wants to pay them. Support this hero today, and I’ll use my cardio for something other than training at 88-94% of my threshold.
