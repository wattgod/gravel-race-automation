Pretty Neat

This is my second GMSR. The last time I came here was in 2018 with my wife and a year-old daughter to try to get my Cat 1 upgrade, which had eluded me for over five years due to personal failings and poor genetics.

While traveling out here, I almost didn’t get to race at all because TSA had torn apart my bike bag, and most of my headset bolts had rattled out. But thanks to super-buzzed mechanics at a closed bike shop who took pity on me and gave me bolts, I got what I needed to have a working bike and start the time trial the next day.

I finished 9th that day, good enough to keep my overall race ambitions alive (and hopefully get enough points to upgrade finally) in a time of 14:40. I thought it was one of the better time trials I’d ever done up to that point.

Today, I finished at 14:16 on the same course, 25 seconds faster than in 2018. Suck it, 2018 Matti!

NGL, it felt great to crank the hog and clap back on the past with a big chungus ride.
