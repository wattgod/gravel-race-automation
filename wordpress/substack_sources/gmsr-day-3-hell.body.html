If you gave me $5, I’d be 75% of the way (even with socialist Vermont taxes) to the six-pack of Miller Lite I’m going to need to feel better about myself after today.

Microstory

Pretty much directly after the comissar’s whistle blew, I immediately got dropped after the race course went up a series of steep hills that made a colonoscopy seem benign, only to crest the top of the climb in a pack of psychos into a screeching downhill filled with charming Vermont 15-foot cracks they mark with bright paint after you pass them. I felt one thing - fuck this.

Look, we were 9 miles into a 75-mile race, and I figured if I wasn’t even fast enough to hang on to the pack with a few lumps, why should I gargle balls for 20 miles trying to catch on, only to get dropped the second we went up something hard again?

So, I gave up chasing the group and just rode easy but steady. Ten minutes in, a group of about seven dudes who were somehow slower than me rolled up in a pace line, and the guy at the front yelled, “GET IN!”

Lol - fuck that. I didn’t even try to accelerate and let them slide by me. The guy who barked at me, a red headed pony tail guy with a anger problems, pulled off the front, dropped to the back while looking at me, and gave me a, ‘WTF?” expression, but I just shrugged.

This might be weird, but I had just had it. I didn’t want to chain gang with the other bottom feeders for the next three hours, taking really stressful pulls, being super careful with my wheel, and trying not to snot rocket or fart at the wrong time. If I was going to suck, I may as well suck without all the stress and turn this ride from a death march tunnel vision skull fuck into a lovely little spin where I may consider even stopping to do a little antiquing or pick up a ham and cheese croissant.

Shoot Me (What sucked today)

Against all odds, I somehow I still caught some people. This filled me with rage.

I wanted to be last place. Unfortunately, in the interest in finishing the stage in less than seven hours, I had to pass them since they were riding so goddamn slow. But if I passed them, they’d probably jump onto my wheel and suddenly I’d be doing exactly what I wanted to avoid doing, which was trading pulls with someone else.

So what did I do? When I got forty feet away from them, I pedaled slower and maintained the distance between us.

This got really weird. At one point I rode for ten minutes behind someone forty feet off their backwheel even though they kept looking back and waiting me to join them and at one point sat up to let me latch on but then I sat up and braked to maintain the distance.

Thankfully, they eventually randomly pulled off into a gas station and as soon I saw them do that I blew a nut and accelerated past them so they had no hope of jumping on my wheel.
